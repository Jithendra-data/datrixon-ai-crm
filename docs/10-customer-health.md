# Customer health v1

Start at 100, subtract observed deductions, floor at zero. No interaction or ≥30 days of silence subtracts 35; 14–29 days subtracts 20. Unresolved critical cases subtract 25 each; other unresolved cases subtract 8. Overdue tasks subtract 5 each, capped at 15. If the previous 30-day window had more than two interactions and the latest window has fewer than half as many, subtract 15.

Dashboard thresholds: healthy ≥75, watch 50–74, at risk <50. Recommendations use <60 for earlier planning. Factors identify source records.

Product adoption and retention predictions are unavailable. Stored synthetic sentiment is not treated as inferred truth. Revenue and renewal dates provide context, not validated retention risk.

Meeting briefs use account evidence and save timestamped CRM memory. Memories can become stale; later generation creates another snapshot rather than rewriting previous evidence.
