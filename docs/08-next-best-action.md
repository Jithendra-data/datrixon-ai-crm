# Next best action

The engine ranks account and opportunity actions by deterministic priority. Risk and missing next steps suggest decision-maker follow-up or close-date review. Health below 60 suggests recovery planning. A separate Follow-Up rule identifies at least 14 days since customer contact.

Records include title, reason, evidence, agent, account/deal links, priority and decision state. They are work suggestions, not assertions of causal revenue impact.

Allowed transitions: pending → accepted/dismissed/snoozed; snoozed → accepted/dismissed/snoozed; accepted → completed. Snoozes last seven days. Acceptance creates one planning task and review request. Terminal decisions survive reruns. Completion is human-reported, not externally verified.

The AI Value view counts generated, accepted, completed and dismissed recommendations, questions and briefs. Acceptance alone is not accuracy. Effectiveness evaluation needs an eligible population, baseline, time windows and rejection reasons. Financial savings are never inferred from counters.
