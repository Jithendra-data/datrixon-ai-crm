# CRM workflows

**Create a deal:** choose an accessible account, name, amount, close date and next action. Validation initializes Discovery, version 1 and pipeline category. Sales authorization is required. Record and audit commit together.

**Review a deal:** inspect its risk factors. Edit stage, date and next action. The submitted version must match. Stage changes append history; won/lost updates forecast category. Stale submissions receive 409.

**Capture activity:** log a meeting, call, email or note from Customer 360. A related opportunity must belong to that account. This is human entry, not external communication. Inbox/calendar capture is a future integration.

**Follow through:** acceptance creates a planning task and independent review request. Approval does not send email or change commercial fields. Snoozing hides an item for seven days; dismissal preserves feedback; completion records a user-reported outcome.

**Improve quality:** run agents, inspect records and acknowledge findings. Resolution requires the underlying rule to stop matching. Automatic merges and record deletion are excluded.
