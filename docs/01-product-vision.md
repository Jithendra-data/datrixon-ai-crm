# Product vision

A focused B2B organization should design its customer operating system around decisions employees actually make. Datrixon puts attention management above form count: who went quiet, which deal lacks evidence, what changed and what should happen next?

The primary persona is a sales manager balancing pipeline quality and follow-through. Executives need a concise grounded brief; representatives need context and next actions; customer success needs relationship and support signals. Analysts need definitions and administrators need control.

Success means a reviewer can follow a number to records, understand a recommendation, decide and find that decision in the audit trail. Recommendation counts are observable; productivity or revenue improvements need an evaluation design and are not claimed.

The scope excludes broad CRM parity, autonomous commercial decisions and inference from unavailable data. The homepage states the thesis; the application makes it inspectable through a synthetic operating scenario.
